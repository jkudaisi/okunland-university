Research centres

Where discovery happens

Established in 2018, The Institute @ OKU brings together interdisciplinary centres, shared core facilities and a technology-transfer office.

Centre for Climate-Smart Agriculture

Drought-tolerant crops, soil health and precision irrigation for Middle Belt smallholders.

Renewable Energy Research Centre

Home of the 1 MW solar micro-grid lab and off-grid energy solutions for rural communities.

Robotics & AI Lab

Autonomous agricultural robots, computer vision and AI for African languages.

Institute of Tropical Medicine

Malaria, sickle-cell disease and maternal health research with OUTH.

Centre for Cultural Heritage Studies

Digitising oral histories, languages and artefacts of the Niger-Benue confluence.

Centre for Governance & Public Policy

Evidence for better policy on security, elections and public finance.

Innovation & patents

From the lab to the field

Automated solar irrigation system

Patented by student Tunde Adeyemi and faculty mentors; now empowering 40 farming cooperatives in North-Central Nigeria.

Paper-based malaria rapid test

A low-cost diagnostic strip that costs under ₦500, in field trials across three states.

Cassava waste bio-briquettes

Clean cooking fuel from agro-waste, licensed to two Kogi State enterprises.

Industry partners can license OKU technologies through the Technology Transfer Office.
