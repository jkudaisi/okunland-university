Welcome to Okunland University

A beacon of rigour, technology and cultural stewardship since 1974

Established in 1974, Okunland University of Nigeria continues to build on its proud legacy as a beacon of academic rigor, technological advancement, and cultural stewardship. We are dedicated to nurturing resilient leaders who impact industries, government, and society.

“Our promise is simple: every student who walks through our gates leaves with knowledge that works, integrity that lasts, and pride in the culture that shaped them.”

Academics

Eight colleges & faculties. One standard of excellence.

From the operating theatre to the courtroom, the robotics lab to the research farm, our colleges prepare graduates for the problems Nigeria and Africa most need solved.

College of Medicine

MBBS, Nursing, Pharmacy and Biomedical Science with a 400-bed teaching hospital.

Faculty of Law

LL.B. with moot court, legal aid clinic and a strong Nigerian Law School pass record.

Faculty of Agriculture

Agronomy, Agricultural Engineering and Food Science on a 120-hectare teaching farm.

Engineering & Robotics

Mechatronics, Electrical, Civil, Computer and Renewable Energy Engineering.

School of Business

Accounting, Finance, Entrepreneurship and an executive MBA for working professionals.

Postgraduate School

PGD, Masters and PhD research degrees across every faculty, full- and part-time.

Campus Visitation & Tours

Experience our sprawling 500-hectare historic landscape in Kogi State.

At Okunland University, we believe that academic brilliance flourishes within a safe, green, and technologically connected environment. From state-of-the-art laboratory complexes and our solar-powered university library to inter-faculty athletic derbies and active student guilds, life on our campus is as vibrant as it is inspiring.

  • Guided tours every weekday at 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM
  • Faculty previews & sit-in lectures for prospective students
  • Campus-wide Wi-Fi and 24/7 solar-backed power in the library

Keystone Honors Academy

Our most selective programme for high-achieving undergraduates. Keystone scholars take interdisciplinary seminars, are paired with a faculty research mentor from first year, and complete a funded capstone project with an industry or government partner.

  • Full or partial tuition scholarships
  • Priority hostel allocation in Heritage Hall
  • Global exchange semester with partner universities
