Scholarships

Over ₦1.2 billion awarded every year

Merit, need-based and endowed scholarships are open to new and returning students. One application on the E-Portal considers you for every award you qualify for.

Vice-Chancellor’s Merit Award

100% tuition for the top 50 UTME/Post-UTME candidates each year, renewable with a CGPA of 4.00+.

Keystone Honors Scholarship

50–100% tuition plus a book allowance for students admitted to the Keystone Honors Academy.

Okunland Heritage Bursary

Need-based support of ₦150,000–₦400,000 for students from low-income households.

Kogi State Indigenes Grant

25% tuition discount for indigenes of Kogi State and host communities, in partnership with the State Government.

Women in STEM Fellowship

Full tuition and mentorship for female students in Engineering, Computing and Physical Sciences.

Sports & Arts Excellence Award

Up to 50% tuition for students who represent OKU in NUGA sports or national arts competitions.

External awards we support: NNPC/Total, PTDF, Agbami, MTN Foundation, Federal Government Scholarship (FSB) and TETFUND.

Loans & Work-Study

More ways to fund your degree

NELFUND student loans

Eligible Nigerian students can apply for interest-free loans from the Nigerian Education Loan Fund to cover tuition and upkeep. Our Financial Aid Office will help you complete your application and verify your enrolment.

Campus Work-Study

Earn ₦30,000–₦60,000 per month working up to 12 hours a week in the library, labs, ICT help desk or Institute research projects.

Instalment plans

Pay 60% at registration and 40% before second-semester examinations, at no extra cost.

How to pay

Safe, cashless payments only

Generate an invoice on the E-Portal

Log in, open Payments, and select the fees you are paying.

Pay via Remita

Pay online by card or bank transfer, or at any commercial bank using your Remita Retrieval Reference (RRR).

Print your receipt

Your receipt appears on the E-Portal within minutes and unlocks course registration.

Beware of fraud. Okunland University never asks you to pay fees into a personal account or through an agent. All payments go through the E-Portal and Remita. Report suspicious requests to info@okunland.edu.ng.
