Plan your visit

Book a campus tour

Guided tours run every weekday at 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM from the Visitors’ Centre at the Main Gate, and last about 90 minutes. Faculty previews and sit-in lectures can be arranged on request.

  • Bring a valid ID for the security check at the gate
  • Free visitor parking beside the Senate Building
  • Wheelchair-accessible tour routes available

Getting here

The campus is on the Kabba-Lokoja Expressway, Kogi State. Lokoja is about 3 hours by road from Abuja; the nearest airport is Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport, Abuja.
