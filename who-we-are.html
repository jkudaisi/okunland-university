About OKU

A national treasure & pillar of heritage

Established in 1974, Okunland University of Nigeria continues to build on its proud legacy as a beacon of academic rigor, technological advancement, and cultural stewardship. We are dedicated to nurturing resilient leaders who impact industries, government, and society.

Today OKU enrols more than 32,000 students across eight colleges and faculties and the Postgraduate School, on a 500-hectare campus on the Kabba-Lokoja Expressway in Kogi State — the confluence state where the Niger and Benue rivers meet.

Our history

Since 1974

1974

Founded by Decree

Okunland University opens on the Kabba-Lokoja corridor with 312 students in three founding faculties: Agriculture, Arts and Natural Sciences.

1977

Faculty of Education

Established to train the teachers needed for Nigeria’s Universal Primary Education scheme.

1979

Faculty of Law

The first law graduates are called to the Nigerian Bar in 1985.

1981

College of Medicine

The College and Okunland University Teaching Hospital (OUTH) admit their first clinical students.

1986

Engineering

The Faculty of Engineering opens; COREN accreditation follows in 1991.

1992

School of Business

Launched with the first part-time MBA in North-Central Nigeria.

2004

Postgraduate School

PhD programmes expand to all faculties.

2012

Solar-Powered Library

The renovated university library becomes one of the first solar-powered academic libraries in Nigeria.

2018

The Institute @ OKU

A dedicated research and innovation institute is established to commercialise university research.

2021

Robotics & Mechatronics Hub

The Faculty is renamed Engineering & Robotics as the Hub opens with TETFUND support.

2024

Golden Jubilee

OKU celebrates 50 years with more than 180,000 alumni worldwide.

2026

Strategic Plan 2026–2031

“OKU at the Frontier” sets targets for research income, digital learning and campus sustainability.

Message from the Vice-Chancellor

Welcome to Okunland

“For fifty years, Okunland has stood for three things: knowledge that solves real problems, integrity that cannot be bought, and pride in the cultures that make us who we are.”

Whether you are a prospective student, a parent, a partner or one of our 180,000 alumni, I am delighted to welcome you. Our graduates lead hospitals, courts, farms, start-ups and ministries across Nigeria and beyond — and more than 94% are employed or in further study within a year of graduation.

As we begin our second half-century, our strategic plan, OKU at the Frontier, commits us to deeper research partnerships, digital and blended learning, and a carbon-neutral campus by 2035. I invite you to visit, to learn with us and to help write the next chapter of our story.

Knowledge

Culture
