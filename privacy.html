Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Okunland University of Nigeria (“OKU”, “we”) respects your privacy and processes personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

What we collect

When you subscribe to The Okunland Gazette we collect your email address and, optionally, your first name and your relationship to the university (student, parent, alumni, staff, partner or other). When you submit a visit or prospectus request we collect the details you enter in the form.

How we use it

We use your details only to send you the Gazette and related university updates, to respond to your request, and to understand which audiences read the Gazette. We do not sell or rent your personal data.

Legal basis

We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the consent box. You can withdraw consent at any time by clicking “unsubscribe” in any email or by contacting us.

Retention

We keep subscriber data until you unsubscribe, after which we delete it within 30 days (keeping only a suppression record so we do not email you again).

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Email info@okunland.edu.ng with the subject “Data Protection”.

Contact

Data Protection Officer, Okunland University, P.M.B. 1002, Kabba-Lokoja Expressway, Okunland, Kogi State, Nigeria. Email: info@okunland.edu.ng.
