Undergraduate study

Learn by doing, from your very first semester

Every OKU bachelor’s degree is accredited by the National Universities Commission (NUC) and, where relevant, by professional bodies including MDCN, COREN, CLE, ICAN, NMCN and PCN. Programmes run on a two-semester calendar with continuous assessment, and include the Students Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES) in your penultimate year.

  • 4–6 year programmes depending on discipline
  • General Studies (GST) courses in communication, entrepreneurship and Nigerian peoples & culture
  • Academic adviser and peer mentor for every first-year student
  • Minors and electives across faculties

College of Medicine

Est. 1981 · Okunland University Teaching Hospital (OUTH), 400 bedsLeadership: Prof. Ifeoma Nwachukwu, Provost

Clinical training starts in year three at our 400-bed teaching hospital and four community health centres across Kogi State. Graduates sit MDCN, NMCN and PCN licensing examinations with pass rates consistently above the national average.

  • MBBS Medicine & Surgery (6 yrs)
  • B.N.Sc. Nursing Science (5 yrs)
  • Pharm.D. Pharmacy (6 yrs)
  • B.Sc. Biomedical Science
  • B.MLS Medical Laboratory Science
  • B.Sc. Physiotherapy
  • B.Sc. Public Health
  • B.Sc. Anatomy
  • B.Sc. Physiology

Faculty of Law

Est. 1979 · Moot Court, Legal Aid Clinic, Law LibraryLeadership: Prof. Adebayo Ogunleye, Dean

Our LL.B. blends Nigerian, common and Islamic law with ADR, technology and human-rights law. Students argue in national moot competitions and serve rural communities through the OKU Legal Aid Clinic before proceeding to the Nigerian Law School.

  • LL.B. Common Law (5 yrs)
  • LL.B. Common & Islamic Law
  • Diploma in Law
  • Certificate in Alternative Dispute Resolution

Faculty of Agriculture

Est. 1974 (founding faculty) · 120-hectare teaching & research farmLeadership: Prof. Hauwa Garba, Dean

OKU’s founding faculty remains the engine of food security research in the Middle Belt, with a seed bank, fish farm, poultry unit and agro-processing pilot plant on campus.

  • B.Agric. Agronomy
  • B.Eng. Agricultural & Bio-resources Engineering
  • B.Sc. Animal Science
  • B.Sc. Food Science & Technology
  • B.Sc. Fisheries & Aquaculture
  • B.Sc. Agricultural Economics & Extension
  • B.Sc. Soil Science

Postgraduate School

Advance your expertise

The Postgraduate School coordinates more than 90 PGD, Masters and PhD programmes across all faculties, with full-time, part-time and weekend study options.

PGD
12–18 months, for graduates switching discipline
Masters
M.Sc., M.A., M.Eng., LL.M., MBA, MPH — 18–24 months
M.Phil/PhD
Research degrees, 3–5 years, with TETFUND and Institute @ OKU funding
Admission
Second Class Lower minimum (Upper for PhD track), NYSC discharge or exemption

Keystone Honors Academy

For the boldest minds

A selective, interdisciplinary honours community of around 120 scholars per year.

  • Small seminars on leadership, ethics and African thought
  • Faculty research mentor from first year
  • Funded capstone with an industry or government partner
  • Exchange semester with partner universities abroad
  • Full or partial tuition scholarships and priority housing

Selection is by UTME and Post-UTME performance (top 5%), an essay and an interview. Current students can apply at the end of 100 Level with a CGPA of 4.50 or above.

University Library

Solar-powered, open late

The main library holds more than 350,000 print volumes and gives access to 60,000+ e-journals (JSTOR, ScienceDirect, HINARI, AGORA, EBSCOhost). A 500 kW solar array keeps reading rooms, Wi-Fi and the 200-seat e-library running through outages.

Hours
Mon–Fri 8:00 AM – 11:00 PM · Sat 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM · 24-hour reading room during exams
Services
Research help desk, inter-library loans, thesis printing, assistive-technology room
